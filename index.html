Audio Beacons

A first-person audio shooter. Targets approach as sound beacons from five positions around you — 9, 10:30, 12, 1:30 and 3 o'clock. Swipe toward a beacon to shoot it before it reaches you.

How to play

Targets appear quietly at one of five clock positions and get louder as they close in. Swipe a single finger across the screen in the direction of a target's position to fire at it — for example, swipe up-and-left toward 10:30, or straight up toward 12. Swipe down to reload.

Some targets take more than one hit. If any target reaches full volume before you destroy it, the run ends immediately, so prioritise whichever target sounds closest.

This game is played with VoiceOver switched off, since its gestures would otherwise conflict with aiming. Every menu, including this one, stays fully VoiceOver-accessible.

Turn VoiceOver off to begin

Aiming uses raw swipe gestures, which VoiceOver intercepts for its own navigation. Turn VoiceOver off now — triple-click the side button (or use your usual shortcut), or switch it off from Control Centre — then choose "I've turned it off" below.

Menus, pause and the end-of-round screen are all VoiceOver-accessible again as soon as you return to them.
