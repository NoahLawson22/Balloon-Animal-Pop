Balloon Animal Pop

An audio-only first-person shooter. Balloon animals approach as sound beacons from five positions around you. Swipe toward one to pop it before it reaches you.

How to play

Balloon animals approach as sound beacons from five positions — 9, 10:30, 12, 1:30 and 3 o'clock. They start quiet and get louder as they close in. Swipe a single finger in the direction of a beacon to pop it, for example up-and-left toward 10:30, or straight up toward 12. Swipe down to reload.

Some animals take more than one hit, and some approach faster than others. If any animal reaches full volume before you pop it, the run ends immediately.

Popping uses raw swipes, so VoiceOver needs to be off for that part. Every other screen, including this one, works by touch and spoken feedback on its own: touch and slide your finger to hear your options, then lift your finger while on one to choose it.

Turn VoiceOver off to begin

Popping balloons uses raw swipe gestures, which VoiceOver intercepts for its own navigation. Turn VoiceOver off now if it isn't already — triple-click the side button, or use your usual shortcut — then choose "I've turned it off" below.

You won't need to turn it back on: every other screen, including this one, works by touch and spoken feedback on its own.
