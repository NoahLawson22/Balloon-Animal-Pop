Balloon Animal Pop

How to play

Balloon animals approach as sound beacons from five positions: 9, 10:30, 12, 1:30 and 3 o’clock. Swipe toward one to pop it before it reaches you. Some take more than one hit, and the run ends the instant any one of them reaches you.

On every screen, including this one, swipe left or right to move between options, and double-tap anywhere to choose one.
